    How this works

    Close passes

    A close pass is when a vehicle overtakes a rider too close. A rear-facing camera on the bike detects it.

    Example data: computer-generated near misses that show how the map will work. Not real rides yet.

    Injuries and lanes

    Cyclists killed or seriously injured from 2022 to mid October 2026, and the City's existing and planned (2025–2027) cycling network, from the City of Toronto. Lane km are the length of line in the City's files cut at ward borders; some streets have one line per direction, so compare wards rather than reading them as exact network length.

    This map does not say lanes cause or prevent injuries. More people ride downtown, so more are hurt there.

    Privacy

    No accounts, no video. The start and end of every ride are trimmed off, and no ride routes are published, only totals per street and ward.
