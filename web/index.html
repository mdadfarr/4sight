How this works

A close pass is when a vehicle overtook a rider too close. A rear-facing camera on the bike detects it and logs where and when.

We add up close passes on each street and divide by how far riders went there, so a busy street isn't red just because more people ride it. The result is close passes per 100 km ridden.

Example data. The close passes on this map today are computer-generated. They show how the map will work. They are not real rides yet. Real rides will be labelled "Real rides", and our own trial rides "Test ride".

Injuries and bike lanes come from the City of Toronto (Vision Zero and the cycling network). Injury counts are not per rider: far more people ride downtown, so more injuries there partly reflects more riding. Nothing here shows that a lane causes or prevents injuries.

Decisions about bike lanes are being made without near-miss data. 4sight is a way to collect it.

Privacy

  • No accounts. Riders are not identified.
  • No video is uploaded.
  • The start and end of every ride are trimmed off, so homes and workplaces don't show.
  • Ride routes are not published, only totals per street.
