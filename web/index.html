      Synthetic data is generated by tools/synth_events.py on real Toronto streets: close passes are three times as likely without a bike lane, more frequent in the commute peaks and on 60 km/h roads. It shows what the city view looks like at scale; it is not a measurement. Real rides are tagged and drawn as solid dots.

      City of Toronto data, shown as published and downloaded October 17, 2026: cyclists killed or seriously injured from the Vision Zero KSI service, bike lanes from the Cycling Infrastructure layer, programmed cycling projects (2025 to 2027) and the 25 wards from Open Data. KSI figures are counts, not rates per rider. Far more people ride downtown, so more injuries there do not show that lanes cause or prevent them; do not read the map that way.

      Streets © OpenStreetMap contributors. Basemap © Esri. No rider identities are stored; the first and last 50 m of every real ride are removed.
